FAQ

Honest answers to common questions

What happens during the 30-day free pilot?

After a free chat, we set up your assistant with your hours, services and booking rules. You listen to test calls and tell us what to change. Then it starts answering real calls for 30 days at no cost, and the $299 setup fee is waived. At the end, you decide whether to continue on a paid plan. If you don't, we switch it off and your calls go back to how they were.

Can I keep my current phone number?

Yes. In most cases you simply forward calls from your existing number (all the time, after hours, or only when you don't pick up). Your customers keep dialing the number they already know. We'll walk you through forwarding for your phone provider.

Does it sound robotic?

Today's AI voices sound much more natural than the phone menus you're used to, but they're not perfect, and some callers will notice they're talking to an assistant. That's why you hear test calls before going live, and why callers can always leave a message or ask to be passed to a person.

What if a caller asks something it can't answer?

It won't make things up. It takes a message, lets the caller know someone will get back to them, and sends you a summary right away so you can follow up. You choose what it should and shouldn't handle.

Will it work with my booking calendar?

It works with many common calendar and booking tools. Since every business is different, we'll check your setup during the free chat and tell you honestly what will and won't connect.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract. You can cancel anytime before your next billing date, and there's nothing to pay if you stop during the free pilot.

Do I need to be good with technology?

Not at all. We do the setup for you. You'll mostly just read call summaries and let us know if anything needs tweaking.
